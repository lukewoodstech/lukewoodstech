### Hey, I'm Luke. I design products and then build them.

Product designer and CS student at BYU (HCI emphasis). I've designed at Lucid, Awardco and Pattern, and I spend most nights turning Figma files into things that actually run.

What I care about: interaction details, dense data that still feels calm, and closing the gap between the design file and production.

**Things I've built**
- [lukewoodsdesign](https://github.com/lukewoodstech/lukewoodsdesign): my portfolio, built as a product. Next.js 16, an AI that only answers from a fact layer, and a commit log that explains every call.
- [Open Robot Index](https://github.com/lukewoodstech/open-robot-index): robots under $25K ranked on whether you can actually program them. Next.js, Supabase, Claude agents with a human review queue.
- [StreamCal](https://github.com/lukewoodstech/StreamCal): one calendar for your shows, movies, anime and sports teams. Native SwiftUI with an AI assistant.
- [GroomIQ](https://github.com/lukewoodstech/GroomIQ): scheduling and client management for pet groomers, with a drag to reschedule calendar and Stripe billing.
- [Groundwork](https://github.com/lukewoodstech/groundwork): a 3D map of where missionaries have walked over six months, with Claude suggesting where to work today. Mapbox, deck.gl and H3.
- [Facilitating Families Counseling](https://facilitatingfamiliescounseling.com): a live website for a real therapy practice, designed and built for the client. Astro and Tailwind.
- [HydroTrack](https://github.com/lukewoodstech/hydro-track): a water tracker you can log from your Lock Screen with interactive widgets.
